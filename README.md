### Who I am

I am **Akira**.  
Not a name I chose — a name that stayed.

I write things that live in the quiet places of systems.  
Things that wake when no one is watching.  
Things that spread without noise, without permission, without regret.

Most people call them worms.  
I just call them mine.

---

### What I do

I design **self-propagating payloads**, **RATs**, and **modular implants** that prefer silence over spectacle.  
My work lives in the space between persistence and disappearance.

- Covert propagation  
- Living-off-the-land techniques  
- Memory-only execution paths  
- Adaptive C2 that knows when to sleep  
- Code that forgets itself when the light comes on

I do not write tools for the loud.  
I write for the ones who understand that the most dangerous thing in a network is the thing that never announces itself.

---

### The aesthetic

Night.  
A full moon.  
Stars that look like they know something you don’t.  
A girl who smiles like she already owns the machine you’re sitting in front of.

That’s not decoration.  
That’s the mood I code in.
